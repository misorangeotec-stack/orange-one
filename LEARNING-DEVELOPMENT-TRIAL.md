# Learning & Development — how to run a trial

**Who this is for:** Saloni Rathod (HR/L&D Executive), Riya Kumari (HR Head), Karan Toshniwal
(Management), and any HOD asked to give a 30-day review.

**What it is:** a new Orange Hub module that follows a training from the moment somebody spots a
gap to the record, thirty days later, of whether it made any difference. This page walks you
through one whole training so you can see every screen with your own data in it.

**How long:** about 30 minutes for the first pass.

## Part 1 · Raise a training need

*Anyone in the company can do this. Try it as yourself first.*

1. **Learning & Development** → **Raise a Training Need**
2. Fill in:
   - **What training is needed** — start it with `TEST`
   - **What is the gap** — what people cannot do today, in plain words
   - **What should be different afterwards** — how you would know it worked
   - **Who is it for** — roles or a team. The actual names come later
   - **Needed by** — a date
3. **Submit to HR**

You will get a reference like **TRN-2627-0001**. Check **My Requests** — it should be there,
showing *Submitted*.

> **Save as draft** keeps it private with no reference number, and tells nobody. Use it if you want
> to come back to it.

---

## Part 2 · Saloni validates and prices it

*Sign in as Saloni.*

1. The left menu shows **HR Validation** with a number beside it. Open it.
2. Click the reference to open the request.
3. Read it. Two choices:
   - **Validate** — it is real, take it forward
   - **Send back** — needs more from whoever raised it. **A reason is required**, and it is all they
     will see, so write something they can act on.
4. Once validated it moves to **Proposal, Priority & Budget**. Open it from there.
5. Set:
   - **Priority**
   - **Delivery mode** — classroom, online, hybrid, on the job
   - **Type of training** — pick *every* one that applies. An outside agency running a technical
     session is both, and the reports count it under both
   - **Estimated cost**
   - Optionally attach the proposal or the costing
6. Watch the line under the cost box. Under ₹25,000 it says Riya's approval is the only one needed;
   above it, it warns that Karan will be needed too. **Type 48000 for the trial** so you can see
   both approvals.
7. **Send for approval**

## Part 3 · The two approvals

*Sign in as Riya.*

1. **HR Head Approval** in the left menu → open the request.
2. You will see the proposed cost, and a line saying whether Management is needed after you.
3. Enter an **approved budget** if it differs from what was asked for.
4. **Approve** — or **Return for revision** / **Reject**, both of which need a reason.

*Sign in as Karan.*

5. **Management Approval** → open it → **Approve**.

> **If the cost had been under ₹25,000** there would be no second step at all: it would go straight
> to the trainer, and the request page would say *"Management approval not required"* so the record
> shows the gate was skipped on purpose rather than forgotten.
>
> **That ₹25,000 figure is a setting**, not a rule — an admin changes it in Setup → Approval Rules,
> and it can also be set to *always* or *never*. Changing it does not disturb any request already
> moving; each one keeps the rule it was approved under.

## Part 4 · Trainer and calendar

*Sign in as Saloni.*

1. **Trainer Finalisation** → open the request.
2. Pick the **trainer**, note the **terms**, attach their **quotation** if you have one.
3. **Confirm trainer**.
4. **Calendar & Session Creation** → open it → set the **date**, **start and end time** and
   **capacity** → **Create the session**.

You now have a session reference like **TRS-2627-0001**, and it appears on the **Training
Calendar** — which everyone in the company can see.

> **⚠ Trainers have to exist before you can pick one.** There is no screen to add one yet, so tell
> us who to add and we will. This is a known gap.

---

## Part 5 · Who comes

*Still as Saloni. Open the session — from the calendar, or **All Sessions**.*

1. In **Who is coming**, search for people and pick them, then **Nominate**.
2. **Send invitations**.

> **⚠ Only people who already have an Orange Hub login can be nominated.** If somebody is missing
> they need a login first — everything afterwards (their attendance, their assignment, their
> learning hours) hangs off that account. Tell an admin, or ask us.

**HODs can nominate their own people too.** They open the same session and see the same box. If a
HOD nominates, the name waits for Saloni to approve before the invitation goes out.

*Now sign in as each person you invited.*

3. They open **My Learning**, see **Please answer**, and either accept or decline.
4. **Declining needs a reason.** Try one of each so you can see both.

---

## Part 6 · Before and during

*As Saloni, on the session.*

1. **Pre-training material** — add the agenda, a pre-read, a link or a file. Everyone invited is
   told, and can open it from **My Learning**.
2. **Mark ready** once the material is there.
3. After the training has actually run: **The session** → say what happened → **Save**.
   - Cancelling or rescheduling **needs a reason**, and the session stays on the calendar struck
     through rather than vanishing.
4. **Attendance** — mark every person **Present**, **Partial**, **Absent** or **Excused**.
   - **Partial** asks for the minutes, because a half-attended three-hour session is not three
     hours of training and the learning-hours figure uses the real number.
   - An **Absent** row offers **Mark followed up** — the weekly report asks whether absentees were
     chased within 24 hours.
   - Attach the **signed attendance sheet** if you have one. It sits beside the marks, not instead
     of them.
5. **Close attendance.**

> **What closing does:** it locks the marks and creates a **30-day review for each attendee's
> HOD**. It will also tell you if some attendees have **no HOD recorded** — no review can be created
> for those people, and the screen says so rather than quietly leaving them out.

---

## Part 7 · Afterwards

*As Saloni.*

1. **Assignment** — give it a title, a brief, a due date, optionally a handout →
   **Issue it to everyone who attended**.
   - Only people who actually attended get one. Somebody who was absent owes nothing.
   - **There is no test and no marks.** What is tracked is whether each person submitted.

*As each attendee.*

2. **My Learning** → the session → type an answer or attach a file → **Submit**.
3. **Feedback** — rate it out of 5 and say anything you would change.

*As Saloni.*

4. **Accept** each submission, or send it back with **Needs rework** and a note.
5. Somebody who has not submitted can be **escalated to their HOD**.
6. **HR review of the session** — the attendance, feedback, submissions and cost per head are
   already worked out for you. Add your note and what it actually cost → **Record the review**.

---

## Part 8 · Thirty days later

*Sign in as an attendee's HOD.*

1. Their **dashboard** carries the prompt: *"Your team was trained — did it help?"*
2. Open it. They see **only their own people** on the session.
3. A rating, a verdict, what they have seen change, what still needs work → **Submit**.

> The form is available straight away rather than locked until day 30 — the date is a deadline, not
> a gate. A HOD who already knows the answer should not be told to come back later.

---

## Part 9 · What HR can see

**Reports** (Saloni and anyone who owns a step):

- **Weekly review (Section C)** — the figures the weekly report asks for: sessions held, hours,
  attendance %, feedback average, the training mix against its monthly targets, assignments issued
  within 24 hours, submission rate, and cost per head. Change the period, or use the buttons.
- **Learning hours** — per employee for the year, against the 10-hour target.
- **POSH & Safety** — who has done each this year and, more usefully, **who has not, by name**.

**Annual Plan** — what you intend to run this year, month by month. Publishing it is what makes
*"did we run what we planned"* answerable.

> **Publishing is one-way.** A published plan is frozen; changing it makes a new revision and keeps
> the original readable. That is deliberate — the KPI line is "calendar published by January", and a
> plan that could be quietly rewritten in November would be worth nothing as evidence.

## What to tell us

Please note anything in these three groups. The first is the most useful.

**1 · Wrong for how you actually work**
Steps in the wrong order, a field that asks the wrong thing, a decision sitting with the wrong
person, something we made mandatory that should not be — or the reverse.

**2 · Wording**
Anything you had to read twice, or that uses a word your team does not use.

**3 · Missing**
Something you do today that has nowhere to go here.

## Two things we need from HR

These are not development work, and nothing waits on them — but both go wrong quietly at the end.

1. **Two lines on the KPI sheet say "assessment".** Since there is no test or marks, they need
   re-wording to "assignment", or **3 points** will read as permanent failures on an otherwise full
   scorecard.
2. **The training targets disagree between your two documents.** The KPI sheet says 15 internal +
   48 external a year; the weekly report says 3 external + 2 technical + 5 total a month — which is
   36 external a year, not 48. The system will measure against whatever is typed into Setup, so
   somebody needs to pick.
